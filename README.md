# Makan Map

Our family's food map of Singapore. You can:

- **Spin** for a stall, or for a food centre when the group wants different things.
- **Log and rate** what you eat.
- **Collect stamps** from every hawker centre.

It installs to your home screen and works offline.

**Open the app:** https://marvyeo.github.io/FoodGuide/

**To install it:**
- iPhone: open it in Safari, tap Share, then *Add to Home Screen*.
- Android: open it in Chrome, open the ⋮ menu, then tap *Install app*.

**To join the family:** sign in with Google and use the invite link. Your Google account must be on the family Sheet and on the app's test-user list.
